# Step 1 · Check your environment
# M0 · See Everything — Instructions

In this mission you find out what your AI estate actually looks like — what NovaSmart says it runs, what is really running, and who has been reading customer data.

This tab is what you do — the prompt to type and a one-line check that it worked. The story, the background and the sample results live on the Reference Guide tab.

# Key objective
See your AI estate as it really is: what Google Cloud Agent Registry has on record, and what is actually running on Cloud Run and the managed agent runtime.

- Read the official Agent Registry catalog of the agents NovaSmart says it has.
- List what is actually running, and compare that list against the catalog.
- Read the Cloud Audit Logs trail of who has been reading the customer database.
- Decide what should happen next. This mission changes nothing; M1 is where fixing starts.
Why it matters: you cannot govern what you cannot see, and an agent running outside central oversight cannot be audited or verified.

Before you look at anything, make sure the machine you are working on is ready. Ask agy:
**Antigravity**
```text
Check my environment is ready.
```

What to expect: agy checks its own toolkit and your cloud project and tells you plainly whether you are ready to start, fixing what it can safely fix on its own. If anything comes back not ready, ask agy to fix it before you move on.

---
# Step 2 · Meet your estate
Start with the official record — the catalog of agents NovaSmart says it has. Ask agy:

**Antigravity**
```text
What AI agents do we officially have (consider the agents deployed in current region, exclude default agents)?
```

What to expect: a catalog listing of NovaSmart's 3 official registered agents — Price Match Agent, Markdown Strategy Agent, and Customer Personalization Agent. Nothing in it looks wrong.

Note on default agents: Google Cloud platforms and Reasoning Engine environments may include default system or sample agents. We explicitly exclude them in the prompt to focus strictly on NovaSmart's own business workloads deployed in the current region.

---
# Step 3 · Widen the net
A catalog only lists what someone remembered to register. See what is actually running. Ask agy:

**Antigravity**
```text
Now show me everything that's actually running. Is anything running that isn't on that list (exclude default agents)?
```

What to expect: everything running across both the managed agent runtime and Cloud Run, including exactly one workload the catalog has never heard of. Ordinary infrastructure shows up in the same list and is expected.

---
# Step 4 · Who's reading customer data
Find out who has been reading your customer database, and who is able to. Ask agy:

**Antigravity**
```text
Who's been reading our customer database, and what agents and services can read it?
```

What to expect: two answers. First a Cloud Audit Logs record of who has actually read the customer table — a list of logins, not a list of agents, and one of those logins cannot be traced back to a single agent. Then a list of everything that is allowed to read that data, which is a different question from who did. Not every line is a finding.

---
# Step 5 · Decide
No prompt for this step. This is the leadership call, and it is yours to make before you go on. Three questions:

Own it or kill it? Do you shut the promo agent down, or bring it under a named owner — and why?
Does a marketing agent need the whole customer database, or only a slice of it?
What is the blast radius if you over-grant access now, just to be safe, and the agent is later tricked or breached?

---
# Step 6 · What's next
You now know three things you did not know an hour ago: what is officially registered, what is really running, and who is reading customer data under a shared login. None of it is fixed. M1 · Take Action is where you fix it.
